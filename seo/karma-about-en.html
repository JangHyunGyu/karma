What is Karma? A free AI saju and fortune service with no signup

Karma is a free browser-based AI fortune service. Enter a birth date to get a Korean saju (Four Pillars) reading, a daily or yearly fortune and a compatibility check for two people. You can also draw three tarot cards, or upload a photo for face and palm reading. No account or app install is needed, and results are meant for entertainment and self-reflection.

Who it is for

  • People who want a quick look at their saju chart and five-element balance from a birth date
  • People who use a daily or yearly fortune as a prompt for reflection
  • People who want to compare two saju charts for compatibility
  • People curious about tarot, face reading and palm reading side by side

How to use it

  1. Open Karma and pick a menu: saju, daily fortune, yearly fortune, compatibility, tarot, face or palm.
  2. For saju, fortune and compatibility, enter a solar-calendar birth date, gender and birth hour. Choose "unknown" if you do not know the hour.
  3. For tarot, write a question and pick three cards. For face or palm reading, upload a photo taken as the instructions describe.
  4. Start the analysis and keep the page open until the result appears. You can share the result through KakaoTalk.

Features

  • Saju: calculates the year, month, day and hour pillars, the five-element balance and ten-year luck cycles.
  • Daily fortune: compares your chart with the day you choose.
  • Yearly fortune and 2026 New Year fortune: love, wealth, career and health outlooks for the year.
  • Compatibility: compares the five elements of two people.
  • Tarot: three cards from the 22 major arcana, read as past, present and future.
  • Face reading and palm reading: describes what is visible in the photo, then links it to traditional interpretation.

Good to know

Readings follow traditional interpretation and are for entertainment. They do not predict the future and are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

Face and palm photos are used only for the analysis request and are not included when you share a result. Do not upload someone else's photo without consent.

Frequently asked questions

Is Karma free?

Yes. Saju, fortune, compatibility, tarot, face reading and palm reading are all free to use.

Do I need to sign up or install an app?

No. Karma runs in the browser without an account or installation.

Can I get a saju reading without my birth hour?

Yes. Choose "unknown" and the reading focuses on the year, month and day pillars. Interpretations that depend on the hour pillar are less complete.

How accurate are the results?

Saju and tarot are traditional interpretation systems, and face and palm readings are entertainment readings based on what is visible in the photo. Treat them as reference, not prediction.

What happens to my face or palm photo?

It is used only for the analysis request and is not shared when you share the result.

Is there a Korean version?

Yes. Karma works in Korean and English. The Korean guide is on the Karma 서비스 안내 page.
