What "face analysis" means here

Many "face analysis" tools measure symmetry, estimate age or score attractiveness. Karma does none of that. Its analysis is interpretive: it states what is visible in the photo, such as the width of the forehead or the shape of the eyes, and links it to the symbolic meaning used in traditional face reading. The result covers themes like personality, relationships and money.

The five zones

  • Forehead: traditionally tied to early life, thinking and how you approach things.
  • Eyes: expression, attention and how you relate to people.
  • Nose: associated with money and self-image.
  • Mouth: communication and appetite for life.
  • Jaw and cheeks: determination and later life.

Each zone gets a score, and the overall score is their average. It is an entertainment indicator.

Run an analysis

  1. Open the face analysis page.
  2. Upload a front-facing photo without hat, sunglasses or mask.
  3. Pick gender and age group, then press Analyze.
  4. Read the zone notes, fortune reading and advice.

Readings follow traditional interpretation and are for entertainment. They do not predict the future and are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

Photos are used only for the analysis request. They are not included when you share a result. Do not upload another person's photo without their consent.
