What the palm reading covers

The reading first describes what is visible: length, depth, curve and breaks in the main lines. It then connects them to traditional palmistry. The life line, head line, heart line, fate line and sun line are covered, along with the overall hand shape. A longer life line does not mean a longer life, and the reading does not diagnose health.

Which hand to photograph

In the tradition Karma follows, the dominant hand shows what you have built through effort and the other hand shows innate tendencies. The tool asks which hand you write with and which hand you photographed, and uses both when it interprets the lines.

Photo tips

  • Spread your palm wide open.
  • Shoot in a bright place so the lines are clear.
  • Take a close-up showing only the palm. Including the fingers helps the hand shape reading.

How to get a free palm reading online

  1. Open the palm reading page.
  2. Upload the photo and choose dominant hand, photographed hand and gender.
  3. Press Analyze and keep the screen open while it works.

Frequently asked questions

Is the palm reading free?

Yes. It is free and needs no signup or app.

Is it a real AI palm reader?

An AI model looks at your palm photo and writes the reading. Palmistry itself is a traditional interpretation system, so treat the result as entertainment.

Which hand should I photograph?

Choose either hand. The tool asks which hand you write with and which you photographed, then reads innate and acquired tendencies accordingly.

How long does it take?

Usually 1 to 2 minutes and up to 3 minutes. Keep the screen open until it finishes.

Does a short life line mean a short life?

No. Line length is not linked to lifespan, and the reading is not a medical diagnosis.
