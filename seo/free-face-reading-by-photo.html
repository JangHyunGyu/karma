What the face reader looks at

Face reading is a traditional practice of linking facial features to character and tendencies. Karma first describes what is visible in your photo, then connects each part to its symbolic meaning. The five zones are the forehead, eyes, nose, mouth, and jaw or cheeks. The overall score is the average of the five zone scores and is an entertainment indicator, not a measurement.

Photo tips for a better reading

  • Use a clear, front-facing photo with your face unobstructed.
  • No hats, sunglasses or masks.
  • Bright, even light with few shadows works best.
  • One person only. Group photos do not work.
  • Heavily filtered or retouched photos can give inaccurate results.

How to get a free face reading online

  1. Open the face reading page.
  2. Upload your photo and choose gender and age group.
  3. Press Analyze and keep the screen open while it works.
  4. Read the zone descriptions, the fortune reading and the advice. You can share the result on KakaoTalk.

Good to know

Readings follow traditional interpretation and are for entertainment. They do not predict the future and are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

Photos are used only for the analysis request. They are not included when you share a result. Do not upload another person's photo without their consent.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. It is free and needs no signup or app.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, bright, front-facing photo of one person without hat, sunglasses or mask.

Is this a face reader that judges looks?

No. It does not rate attractiveness. It describes visible features and relates them to traditional face reading, and the score is an entertainment indicator.

How long does it take?

Usually 30 seconds to 1 minute, and up to 2 minutes. Keep the screen open until it finishes.

How accurate is it?

Face reading is a traditional interpretation system. Treat the result as reference or entertainment, not as a prediction.

Can I use someone else's photo?

Only with their consent. The photo is not included when you share a result.
